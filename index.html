Whot

The Nigerian card game for 2 to 4. Be the first to play your last card.

You need to be signed in with start.ng. Make a room, send the code to up to 3 friends, add bots to fill empty seats, and start. The server deals and checks every card.

    How to play

    Play a card that matches the top card in shape or number, or pick one from the market. A Whot card goes on anything and names the shape. 1 hold on: play again. 14 general market: everyone else picks one, you play again. 8 suspension: the next player misses a turn. 2 pick two and 5 pick three: the next player picks up unless they play another 2 (or 5), which adds on.
